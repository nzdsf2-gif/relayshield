The Attack That Doesn't Need Your Password

There is a second, more sophisticated threat that most security tools cannot detect — and that bypasses 2FA entirely. Infostealer malware runs silently on your device. No ransom demand. No slowdown. No warning. Within minutes of infection it extracts every saved password, every active session cookie, every stored credit card from your browser. Those logs are packaged and sold on darknet markets — typically within 48 to 72 hours of infection.

The attacker does not need your password. They do not need your 2FA code. They use your session cookie — a token your bank, email provider, and business tools issued when you last logged in. From their perspective, they are you. Authenticated. No alerts. No friction.

How the attack unfolds
Hour 0 Device silently infected. No visible sign.
Hrs 1–4 All passwords, session cookies, and credentials extracted.
Hrs 24–72 Credentials appear in darknet log sold for $10–$50. Still no warning to you.
Day 3–14 Attacker uses your session cookie. Authenticated. No 2FA required.
Week 2+ Fraudulent transfer clears. Client calls about suspicious email from your address.
