# RelayShield Order Screening is Live on the Shopify App Store

Shopify merchants can now screen every order against live threat intelligence, right from the App Store.

## The problem: agent orders are coming, and fraud follows

Shopify just enabled Poppy, the Personal Agent Protocol, for every merchant. That means AI agents can now discover your products and place orders on behalf of customers. It is a new sales channel, and a new attack surface.

A compromised agent, a stolen credential, or a prompt injection can turn an agent order into fraud. The order looks normal. The buyer looks normal. But the payment is stolen, the address is a drop, or the account was taken over. By the time you find out, the goods are gone.

## What RelayShield does

RelayShield Order Screening checks every order against our live threat-intelligence corpus, built from 144 monitored Telegram marketplaces, 850K+ indicators, and 11M+ citations.

- High-risk orders are tagged automatically (relayshield-high-risk), so you can see them at a glance.
- Medium-risk orders are flagged for review (relayshield-review), with a timeline note showing the score and the reasons.
- Each flagged order carries the evidence, so you can decide with confidence.

RelayShield never blocks, cancels, or refunds an order on its own, and never calls an order safe. You stay in control. We give you the signal.

Privacy-first: customer emails are SHA-256 hashed, never stored raw, with 90-day retention.
