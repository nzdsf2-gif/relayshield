# Crypto Shield Mobile — Marketing Intern Plan

Welcome aboard. This is a working plan for building brand visibility and driving
traffic/engagement for RelayShield and Crypto Shield Mobile. Everything here is
either ready to submit as-is, or has the content you need included directly —
ask before publishing anything that isn't already drafted below.

**Already done:** Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, Solana dApp Store
(live and discoverable — search "Crypto Shield" in the Solana dApp Store app
on-device).

---

## 1. Directory / listing submissions

These are free, mostly form-filling, and each is real backlink + discovery
value. Recommended next, in priority order:

### Trustpilot (do this first)

**How signup works:** Trustpilot is NOT a "log in with your website" system —
you create a business account with an email + password (use an
@relayshield.net address if you have one, otherwise relayshieldadmin@gmail.com
is fine), then separately verify domain ownership (usually via a confirmation
email or a short verification step in the dashboard). Two steps, not one.

**Business profile content to use:**
- **Business name:** Crypto Shield (by RelayShield)
- **Website:** https://cryptoshieldmobile.relayshield.net
- **Category:** Security Software / Mobile App
- **Description:**
  > Crypto Shield is read-only wallet security monitoring for Solana, EVM
  > (including Base), TON, Bitcoin, and XRP — built by RelayShield, a threat
  > intelligence company protecting businesses and consumers against breaches,
  > SIM-swap fraud, and infostealer malware. Crypto Shield never asks for your
  > seed phrase or private keys and can't move your funds — it watches your
  > wallets and alerts you the moment something looks wrong: address
  > poisoning, malicious NFT contracts, phishing sessions, and SIM-swap/breach
  > exposure tied to your linked accounts. Unlike wallet-security tools that
  > only watch on-chain activity, Crypto Shield treats the credential layer
  > and the chain layer as one attack surface, since most real attacks start
  > off-chain long before a malicious transaction is ever signed.
- **Logo:** ask Dad for the app icon file (512x512 PNG)

## 2. Review generation

Once Trustpilot is live, the highest-leverage next step is getting real reviews
on it (a directory listing with zero reviews doesn't help much). Draft a
short, honest ask — don't write fake reviews or incentivize positive-only
reviews (both against Trustpilot's terms and just bad practice). Ask Dad
before sending anything to real users.

## 3. Social content

- Turn existing blog/Substack posts into short LinkedIn posts (1 idea per
  post, not a re-post of the whole article).
- Simple graphics in Canva — screenshots of the app, a stat or two, consistent
  color scheme (ask Dad for brand colors / existing screenshots in
  `crypto-shield-app/store-assets/`).

## 4. Community engagement

Helpful participation (not spam) in relevant subreddits/Discords about crypto
security and scam awareness. Always disclose you're with RelayShield. Good
communities to start in: r/CryptoCurrency (careful — strict self-promo
rules), general crypto-security Discords. Ask before posting anything that
links back to RelayShield products.

### Solana-specific channels (highest overlap with our actual users)

Since Crypto Shield launched on the Solana dApp Store, these are worth more
of your time than generic crypto communities — same disclosure/ask-before-
posting rule applies:

- **r/solana** — general Solana ecosystem subreddit, helpful participation only.
- **Solana Discord (security/dev channels)** — same rule, don't drop links cold.
- **Superteam** (superteam.fun) — the highest-profile organic amplification
  channel in the Solana ecosystem: a large community/talent network that runs
  bounties and regularly amplifies ecosystem projects through its own
  channels. This is active community engagement, not a static form — check
  superteam.fun directly to see their current engagement process before
  treating this as ready-to-execute.

## 5. Analytics reporting

A simple weekly snapshot (Google Analytics + whatever social platforms show
natively) so Dad can see what's actually moving traffic. Doesn't need to be
fancy — a running doc or spreadsheet with a few rows per week is enough.

## 6. Longer-term ideas (once the above is running)

- App Store Optimization: keyword research for the Google Play / Solana dApp
  Store listing.
- Micro-influencer outreach: small crypto-security YouTubers/newsletter
  writers who might cover the app.
- Product Hunt-style launch prep for any new feature releases (not just the
  initial listing).

**When in doubt, ask Dad first** — especially before posting anything public,
