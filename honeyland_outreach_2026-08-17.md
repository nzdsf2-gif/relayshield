## Original route reasoning, kept for the Step 2 message



The screenshot shows `#🤝|collab-request` with a lock icon and `#🚨|scam-alert`.

**Use `#collab-request`.** A designated channel is consent by construction; a DM is an interruption

removed from a server that would otherwise have said yes.

## What not to do

- **No invite link in the first message.** The ask is permission. Send the link only after a yes,
  otherwise the message is advertising regardless of how it is worded.
- **No claim it would have caught any specific scam** they posted in `#scam-alert`. We have not
  tested that, and an admin who checks and finds otherwise ends the conversation.
- **No urgency, no offer that expires.** Nothing about this is time-sensitive to them.
